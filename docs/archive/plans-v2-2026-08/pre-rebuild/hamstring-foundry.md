# Hamstring Foundry

**Program ID:** `hamstring-foundry` · **Source:** [src/data/plans/hamstringFoundry.ts](../../src/data/plans/hamstringFoundry.ts)
**Duration:** 10 weeks · **Frequency:** 4 days/week — hamstrings 3× + 1 upper recovery day

## Overview

Hamstrings through **three functions**: heavy hip extension (RDL, double +5 kg), knee flexion (curls with **4s eccentric** in Forging), lengthened control (hip-supported DB deadlift). Day 3 is upper-dominant with **no hard hamstring work** by design.

### Weeks 1–5 (5 weeks)

#### Heavy Hip Extension · Forging (dow 1)

- Barbell Romanian Deadlift · 4×5-8 · rest 210s · — Hips back, bar against the legs. Stop where the hamstrings stop, not where the back rounds.

- Flat DB Press · 3×8-12 · rest 120s
- Hammer Upper Row · 3×8-12 · rest 120s

## Phases & week-to-week progression

### Forging (weeks 1–5)
Any `ham-curl` slot forced to tempo `40X0`.

### Tempering (weeks 6–10)
RDL → **5×4-6**; curl slots clear tempo and move to `6-10` reps.

## Techniques, supersets, finishers

- Tempo `40X0` on curls (Forging) and on day-2 seated curl baseline.
- Lengthened DB deadlift notes progression ladder of tempo/pauses once 2×50 kg is easy.
- No supersets.
