# Event Horizon

Twelve weeks, four days, upper/lower hypertrophy. The training itself is
ordinary on purpose — 18–24 sets a session, double progression, effort rising
across the phases. The plan exists for what happens when a region starts
complaining.

The athlete reports a region as `normal`, `strained` or `impaired`. That report
is authoritative: `impaired` always produces a recommendation, and `normal`
never does.

Every recommendation shows the planned movement, each replacement, the role the
replacement preserves, the cost before and after, and the tradeoffs of taking
it. Where no single movement preserves the role cheaply, the plan may split it
into two lighter ones — a cheaper compound for the pattern plus an isolation for
the muscle — and the sets divide rather than double. Where nothing helps, it
says so instead of inventing an option.

Nothing is applied without confirmation. A report on its own changes no session.

Stimulus preservation is stated as rules, not as an invented percentage score.
Costs come from the documented 0–4 ordinal metadata.

Personal learning is bounded. After three comparable exposures agree, a cost may
move by exactly one ordinal step from the expert value, and no further, however
many exposures accumulate.

Accepted swaps are followed up: did the region settle, and did performance hold?
The verdict is recorded honestly as helped, mixed, or did not help.

Verification: `npm run verify:event-horizon`.
