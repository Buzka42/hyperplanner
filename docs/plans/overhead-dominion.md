# Overhead Dominion

Ten weeks of shoulder specialisation, four days a week on a 20X0 tempo. Delts
are trained four times weekly, upper back three times, everything else twice.
The four shoulder exposures are deliberately different in function rather than
four maximal presses: a strict standing military press on day 1, a high-volume
cable lateral and reverse pec deck day, a braced one-arm dumbbell press
(weaker side first, stronger side matches), and a structural day built on
external rotation and rear delts.

The standing press is the progression anchor — 5×5–8 on double progression at
2.5 kg, strict enough that bent knees end the set. For the first five weeks
(Bombardment) that is the whole story. In the second block (Artillery, weeks
6–10) the press converts to two 5/3/2 waves per session, keeping frequency
while shifting the loading character. Everything else in the plan runs
straight sets at moderate reps.

The source concept under-delivers its own minimums, so the plan patches them:
day 2 adds a hammer chest-press slot and day 4 adds incline dumbbell press and
a second triceps exposure, bringing chest and triceps to the two weekly
exposures the concept doc promises but its own day lists miss.
