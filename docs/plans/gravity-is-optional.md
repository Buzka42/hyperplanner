# Gravity Is Optional

Twelve weeks, four days a week, of weighted calisthenics plus hypertrophy work.
Vertical pulling and the dip family appear three times weekly, arms two to
three times, quads and posterior chain twice. The distinguishing mechanic is
total system weight: the weighted chin-up and weighted dip are typed
`weighted-bodyweight` in the library, so load is tracked as bodyweight plus
added plates rather than as an external lift.

Day 1 is the strength day — 5x3–5 on both weighted lifts with 3.5-minute rests
and double progression, where five clean reps on all five sets earns +2.5 kg.
Day 3 replaces sets with total-rep targets: 40 chin-ups and 50 dips accumulated
in as few sets as possible (capped at 12), and the thing you beat next time is
the set count, not the rep count. Day 4 handles the dumbbell deadlift's 50 kg
ceiling with tempo — a four-second eccentric buys difficulty load cannot.

Phases move one variable at a time. Ascent (weeks 1–4) is the base spec.
Escape Velocity (5–8) adds a set to the two weighted lifts and nothing else,
keeping total fatigue flat. Orbit (9–12) raises the total-rep targets by 25%.
