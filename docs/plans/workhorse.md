# Workhorse

**Program ID:** `workhorse` · **Source:** [src/data/plans/workhorse.ts](../../src/data/plans/workhorse.ts)
**Duration:** 10 weeks · **Frequency:** 4 days/week — back 3×, biceps 3×

## Overview

Treats **weighted chin-up** as a major strength lift progressed on **total system weight** (BW + belt), double +2.5 kg, **210 s** rest (Poliquin 3–4 min phosphagen window). Week 10 is the **Chin-Up Trial**.

## Phases & week-to-week progression

### Ascent (weeks 1–4)
Weighted chin 6×3-5 @ 210s rest.

### Overhang (weeks 5–9)
Weighted chin → fixed `3` reps × 6 sets.

### Chin-Up Trial (week 10)
Chin → **3×1-3** with note to pick weighted 3RM *or* BW max reps; other slots −1 set (floor 2).

## Techniques, supersets, finishers

- No supersets.
- Default tempo `20X0`.
- Pronated pull-up volume day for grip variety.
