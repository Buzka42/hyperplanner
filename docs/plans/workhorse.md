# Workhorse

Ten weeks of four days (Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri) of chin-up and back specialisation —
the upper-body squat. Back is trained three times a week, biceps three times,
everything else twice. The weighted chin-up is treated as a major strength
lift: day one opens with 6 sets of 3-5, progressed on total system weight
(bodyweight plus the belt) via double progression at 2.5 kg.

The 3:30 rest on the weighted chin-up is deliberate. Poliquin prescribes 3-4
minutes between 3-5-rep strength exposures for near-complete phosphagen
recovery, and 210 seconds sits mid-window.

The weeks split into three phases. Ascent (1-4) is the base block. Overhang
(5-9) locks the chin-up at 6 sets of 3 while the rest of the days run
unchanged. Week 10 is the Chin-Up Trial: the chin-up becomes 3 sets of 1-3 —
work to a weighted 3RM or test bodyweight max reps, pick one and record it —
and every other slot sheds a set, floored at two.
