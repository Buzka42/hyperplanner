# Project Chimera

Sixteen weeks, four days, upper/lower, in four four-week blocks. The base
programme is balanced across six qualities — squat, hinge, push, pull,
unilateral and hypertrophy — and every quality clears its weekly minimum before
any adaptation is considered.

After each block the plan may propose a mutation. The constraints are the point:

- at most **two weekly sets per quality per block**, moved rather than created —
  what one quality gives up, another takes on;
- every quality keeps a **minimum weekly exposure**, enforced in the engine and
  again at session build, so a stale status cannot strip a quality out;
- **insufficient data means no mutation** — fewer than three comparable
  exposures is not evidence, and the athlete is told that nothing changed;
- every component is **previewed and separately confirmable**, so the athlete can
  accept the set reallocation and decline the exercise change.

A stalled quality with real evidence behind it earns a proposed exercise change,
also confirmed on its own. Investment goes either to what is responding fastest
or to what is clearly behind.

A phenotype label appears after each block. It is a description shown for
interest, it says so in its own caveat, and it is not an input to any decision
the plan makes.

Verification: `npm run verify:project-chimera`.
