# Quadfather

Ten weeks of quad specialisation across four days. Quads are trained three
times; everything else is maintained twice, including one day with no quad work
at all.

Every quad slot carries one of three roles, and each quad day carries at least
two of them:

- **Load** — Hack Squat (default), Barbell Squat, Stiletto Squat, Leg Press.
- **Unilateral/Depth** — split squats, knee-over-toe work, step-ups, deficit
  reverse lunges, heel-elevated goblet squats.
- **Knee-extension/Burn** — Leg Extension, Supported Sissy Squat, Reverse
  Nordic and Stripper Squat.

Stripper Squat is a burn movement and stays one however heavy it is loaded; the
week 8–9 heavy phase only touches load-role movements.

Hack Squat is the default main load for everyone. Free squatting is advised for
short and regular proportions, and the Stiletto Squat is offered first to
long-limbed athletes — all three remain selectable, and the choice is made once
per run.

Range of motion is confirmed by the athlete. Approved variations whose setup
answers the question infer their own depth, and a confirmation always beats an
inference. An unconfirmed slot asks rather than assumes.

Knee feedback (`normal` / `strained` / `impaired`) may replace main or accessory
work. A proposal only ever offers a movement in the same role with a lower knee
cost, and it is applied only after the athlete accepts it. Where no lower-cost
option exists, `impaired` feedback says so and points at a trainer or
physiotherapist.

The dashboard reports role balance and confirmed range of motion, not an
imitation squat total.

Verification: `npm run verify:quadfather`.
