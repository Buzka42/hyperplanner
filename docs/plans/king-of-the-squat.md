# King of the Squat

Twelve weeks of squat-specialisation powerlifting, four days a week. The squat
appears three times weekly — a heavy wave day, a paused volume day, and a
front-squat structural day — bench twice, deadlift once. The deadlift is fixed
at 57.5% for three sets of three and never progressed: it exists to practise
setup and bar speed while spinal and systemic fatigue stays budgeted for
squatting.

The heavy day runs two waves per session. Weeks 1–3 wave 5/4/3, weeks 4–6
wave 4/3/2, weeks 7–9 wave 3/2/1, each wave heavier than the last, with
loading stepping 2.5% of squat 1RM per week off a 75% base. The support lifts
are percentage-anchored too: paused bench at 85–87.5%, paused volume squats at
67.5%, front squats at 60% with reps kept at 3–6 so the upper back never folds
before the legs do. A safety-bar squat is the sanctioned front-squat
alternate.

Realisation (weeks 10–11) cuts the heavy day to 4×2 and pulls a set off
everything else. Test Week (week 12) reduces the squat to three singles with
the instruction to work up to a single the lifter is certain of, while
accessories drop to one or two sets. There is no built-in deadlift or bench
test — only the squat gets a peak.
