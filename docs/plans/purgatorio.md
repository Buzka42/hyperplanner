# Purgatorio

Twelve weeks of four days (upper/lower, Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri), built as two
repeating six-week blocks: three weeks of accumulation followed by three of
intensification. Every major muscle gets at least two weekly exposures in both
states, and antagonist pairing (A1/A2) runs through every session.

The plan is one set of days plus two phase transforms rather than two separate
programmes. Accumulation adds a set to every slot, rewrites rep targets to
10-15, cuts rest to 75% (rounded to 15-second boundaries), and applies a 30X0
tempo where none is set. Intensification rewrites rep targets to 5-8, stretches
rest to 140%, and strips the tempo. AMRAP and Failure targets survive both
transforms untouched.

The four days are fixed: Upper A and Lower A on Monday and Tuesday, Upper B and
Lower B on Thursday and Friday. The B days rotate the variations — incline
press for flat, front squat for hack squat — so the second exposure is
recognisable rather than identical.
