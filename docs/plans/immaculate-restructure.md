# Immaculate (Re)Structure

Ten weeks of four structural-balance sessions a week, alternating Upper/Lower
Structural A and B days on a 20X0 tempo. Every major muscle group is trained
at least twice weekly, and the upper-body days are built around the Poliquin
structural-balance ratios: close-grip bench is the reference lift at 100%,
with weighted chin total system weight at ~81%, incline barbell at ~83%,
preacher curl ~46%, reverse curl ~30%, and single-arm external rotation ~9%.
Those targets live on the library entries via `strengthRef`, not in the plan
file. They are programming references, never safe/unsafe thresholds or injury
prediction.

The plan runs three phases. Assessment (weeks 1–2) establishes where the
ratios actually sit, Correction (weeks 3–7) does the bulk of the work, and
Re-Test (weeks 8–10) drops a set from every slot so the retest measures
strength rather than accumulated fatigue. Weak-link exposure is always a small
third dose of the lagging structure — external rotations and rear-delt flyes
appear on both upper days — never an extra fatigue-heavy compound.

Two departures from the source concept are deliberate. Upper Structural B adds
a direct triceps slot that the concept doc omits, because without it triceps
would see one weekly exposure against the plan's own two-exposure minimum.
Lower A keeps front-squat reps at 3–6 so the torso is never the limiter, with
a safety-bar squat as the sanctioned alternate.
