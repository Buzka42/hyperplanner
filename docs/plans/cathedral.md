# Cathedral

Ten weeks of chest specialisation across four days. Chest is trained three
times and each exposure leads with a different arch; the fourth day is lower
body, so the second chest exposure is not a triceps fatigue test.

The Three Arches:

- **Press** — Incline Dumbbell Press is the heavy arch; Flat Dumbbell Press and
  30° Smith Incline are approved.
- **Stretch** — Dips below parallel and cable flyes.
- **Adduction** — Pec Deck and Chest Crossover.

There is no barbell bench anywhere in the plan. Smith incline is the approved
alternative for anyone who wants a bar under their hands.

Balance is a ratio, not a fixed count: no arch may carry less than half of the
largest. Total chest volume moves across the phases, so a hard-coded floor would
drift out of date.

Machine Press/Fly Combo has its own history and is distinct from both Chest
Press and Pec Deck. It is opt-in, and the athlete assigns it to either the Press
or the Adduction arch.

When something other than the pecs limits pressing twice within three weeks, the
plan offers to move two pressing sets into stable adduction work. The offer
requires confirmation, and the sets move rather than disappear — weekly chest
volume is preserved while the triceps and front delt get relief.

The dashboard combines the arches into one chest profile while still showing
which factor limited the work.

Verification: `npm run verify:cathedral`.
